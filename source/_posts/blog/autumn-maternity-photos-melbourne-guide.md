## The best timing for autumn maternity photos

Most clients schedule maternity photos in the late second to early third trimester, typically between weeks 28-34. Your bump has a defined shape, you usually still feel comfortable moving, and we have flexibility if Melbourne weather shifts.\* Is 20 weeks too early? Not always. If you are carrying a smaller bump or want a subtle silhouette, 20-24 weeks can be lovely. We will lean on movement and angles to shape the bump. If you are having twins or showing earlier, earlier timing can also be practical.

- Is 37 weeks too late? It can still work, but comfort and flexibility matter more. We would keep locations close to parking or choose an in-home session so you can rest between sets. The key is listening to your body and building a plan with buffer time in case baby has other ideas.Autumn-specific note: golden hour arrives earlier as the season progresses. In March, sunset often sits near school-run time; by May, it can be late afternoon. If evenings feel late for little ones, we can aim for sunrise light or choose an in-home session with window light that mimics golden tones.If you are looking to book a session this autumn, limited golden-hour dates tend to go first. Reach out early if a golden-hour evening feels right for you.

## In-home calm or golden-hour outdoors

Choosing between in-home and outdoor is really about energy. Both create beautiful, natural images, and in autumn you can even pair them.\* In-home sessions: Cosy, unrushed and weather-proof. We work with the light you live in, usually near windows, on the bed or by the cot you have been dreaming over. Outfit changes are easy. If you have toddlers, they can take a break for snacks or a book. For cooler days and earlier sunsets, in-home often feels just right.

- Golden-hour outdoor sessions: Parks, gardens and bayside paths glow in autumn. Think tan grasses, russet leaves and soft skies. Sunset is gentle at this time of year, so we avoid squinting, and the air feels calm. We will choose a single, easy-to-walk location and keep a warm layer handy for the last ten minutes as the temperature drops.A lovely hybrid for autumn is 30 minutes at home followed by a quick stroll at the nearest park. ## What to wear for autumn maternity photos

Comfort first, always. Choose pieces that move and skim, with textures that feel like the season.\* Dresses: Wrap dresses, empire waists and soft, stretchy knits flatter a bump and photograph beautifully when you walk or sway. Midaxi lengths avoid tripping on grass.

- Layers: Cardigans, light coats, knits and shawls give shape and warmth. A longline cardigan over a fitted dress creates a lovely silhouette.

- Tones: Soft palettes suit autumn light. Think oatmeal, taupe, sage, dusty rose, rust, charcoal and cream. Coordinate 2-3 tones across the family rather than matching.

- Fabrics: Linen, cotton, chiffon and fine knits add texture without bulk. Avoid stiff fabrics and heavy logos.

- Footwear: Comfortable flats or ankle boots you can walk in. If we head outdoors, closed toes help on paths and lawns.

- Accessories: Simple jewellery and a single hat or scarf if it feels like you. Keep patterns minimal to let your faces shine.If you want more styling guidance, I share a pre-session outfit guide and can help you choose pieces that work with your location and the cooler season.

## Melbourne light, weather and comfort tips for autumn

- Expect cooler evenings: pack a warm layer for everyone, even on mild days. We will use it between shots and for the last five minutes after the sun dips.

- Shorter days help little ones: sunset is earlier, so evening golden hour can work around bedtime.

- Watch the wind: parks with tree lines or gardens with hedges help block chilly breezes; I will position you with the wind at your back so hair moves naturally.

- Keep to one location: autumn light moves quickly. A single, easy-to-access spot lets us follow the glow without stress.

## Quick answers to common questions

- What month of pregnancy is best for maternity photos? Most people feel and look their best between weeks 28-34. The exact week depends on your bump and comfort.

- When should you book a maternity photoshoot? Book after your 12-week scan and aim to lock a date for weeks 28-34, with a weather back-up if we are outdoors.

- Is 20 weeks too early for maternity photos? Not necessarily. If you prefer a subtler bump or you are showing early, 20-24 weeks can be lovely. We will shape the silhouette with movement and light.

- Is 37 weeks too late for maternity photos? It can still be beautiful if you feel comfortable. Choose an in-home session or a short, easy-walk outdoor location and keep the schedule flexible.

- What to wear for a maternity photoshoot? Choose comfortable pieces that move: wrap or empire-line dresses, soft knits and layers in muted tones. Coordinate 2-3 colours across the family and avoid busy patterns.

- Should my partner be in maternity photos? Yes if it feels right to you. Partners add connection and context to your story. We use gentle prompts so it never feels posed.
