## What lifestyle photography is, and how it differs from traditional studio portraits

Lifestyle photography is a guided, documentary-influenced approach that celebrates your real life. Instead of sitting stiffly and holding a smile, you move, chat and cuddle while I guide you with gentle prompts. We still aim for flattering angles and beautiful light, but we embrace natural expressions, movement and the sweet in-between moments.

Traditional studio portraits typically rely on posed setups, controlled backdrops and more formal direction. Lifestyle sessions lean on natural light, meaningful settings and a flow that responds to your family's energy. Think walking hand in hand through a Melbourne park at golden hour, reading a favourite book on the couch, or settling your newborn in your arms while the kettle hums in the background.

## What a lifestyle photographer does on the day

My role is part photographer, part calm guide. I:

- Find or create flattering natural light, whether that is a north-facing window at home or a soft, golden sky in the park.
- Offer simple prompts that help you connect. I might say, _Walk toward me and whisper what you are looking forward to this autumn_, or _Count baby's toes together_.
- Pace the session around your family. We pause for water, snacks, nappy changes and cuddles. Newborn sessions are always baby-led and safety-first.
- Anticipate little, fleeting gestures, like the scrunch of a toddler's nose or the way your partner brushes hair off your face.

The goal is not performance. It is connection. I keep things relaxed so you can be yourselves.

## How sessions flow at home and outdoors

In-home sessions are slow and personal. I arrive, we chat, and I take a quick look around to find the best light. We often start in the main bedroom or living room, then move wherever the light leads. Newborns are photographed mostly in arms, on the bed or in a cosy bassinet, with time for feeds and settling. If siblings or pets are involved, I include them early while energy is high and attention is fresh.

Outdoor sessions lean into movement and atmosphere. Late summer and autumn evenings in Melbourne are ideal, with warm tones and softer sun. We might wander a leafy trail, play a simple game, or share a quiet cuddle as the sun dips. Mornings can be beautiful too if your little ones are early risers. I choose angles that avoid squinting and adapt to wind and sun so you do not need to worry about the elements.

## Feeling awkward in front of the camera

Almost everyone tells me, _We are awkward_, or _We never know what to do with our hands_. That is normal. You will never be left wondering where to look or how to stand. I guide you into natural positions that feel like you, then give you simple actions that create genuine expressions, like bumping hips as you walk or sharing a little secret.

Kids do not have to sit still. In fact, their wiggles bring images to life. We plan around naps and snacks, keep expectations gentle and welcome the chaos as part of the story. Parents often say they forgot the camera was there within minutes.

## Why storytelling images age so beautifully

Trends come and go, but the way your partner's hands rest on your bump, the way your newborn curls into you, and the way your child's hair catches the evening light will always feel like home. Lifestyle images age well because they are grounded in truth. They show your family as you are, in places that matter, with expressions that will still make sense in twenty years.

These photographs become part of your family's language. When your children ask, _What was I like then_, you will have more than a picture. You will have a story you can hold.

## Late summer into autumn in Melbourne

This is a lovely season for sessions. Evenings are warm without the peak heat, and parks show early autumn colour. For little ones with earlier bedtimes, we can choose a soft morning. For sunset lovers, we plan for the hour before sunset to get that honeyed light. Bring layers you can add or remove as the breeze shifts, and keep footwear comfortable so you can move and play.
