## When to book and the best month for photos

Most clients book their maternity session for the late second to early third trimester, typically weeks 28 to 34. Your bump usually has a lovely, defined curve and you can still move comfortably.

- Want a subtler silhouette or you are showing early? Weeks 20 to 24 can work beautifully.
- Feeling good a little later? We can photograph up to 37 weeks if that suits your body and energy.
- Carrying multiples or experiencing earlier discomfort? Let’s bring the session forward so you stay comfortable.

Booking a date 6 to 10 weeks ahead is a safe bet, especially if you prefer a weekend or specific location. If your due date window shifts or the weather turns wild, I keep room to move so we can reschedule with minimal fuss.

## Where to shoot in Melbourne

You can choose in-home for a quiet, intimate feel, or outdoors for sweeping light and space. I will help shortlist locations that suit your style and the season.

- **In-home:** Perfect for rest breaks, easy outfit changes and consistent comfort. We follow the light through your favourite rooms and keep things cosy.
- **Parks and gardens:** Gentle greenery and filtered light suit wind-sensitive days, with easy parking and short walks.
- **Coast and bayside:** Soft horizons, sea air and beautiful movement in fabrics. On windy days we tuck behind dunes or choose a sheltered cove.

For outdoor sessions, I recommend golden hour, either early morning or the hour before sunset, for warm, flattering light and relaxed eyes. In summer, a shaded park or coastal spot keeps everyone cool; in autumn, earlier sunsets and soft colour help create a calm, layered look. ## What to wear for a maternity photoshoot

Choose pieces that move, breathe and flatter your bump without constant adjusting.

- **Fabrics:** Linen, cotton, soft knits, chiffon and fine rib knits drape well and photograph softly.
- **Silhouettes:** Wrap dresses, empire waists, body-skimming midi dresses or a stretchy knit two-piece that highlights your shape without clinging sharply.
- **Tones:** Coordinate 2 to 3 complementary muted or mid tones across the family rather than matching. Think oat, soft sage, clay, misty blue, warm neutrals. Avoid bright neons and heavy logos.
- **Layers:** A light cardigan, denim jacket or open knit adds texture and options if the breeze picks up.
- **Footwear:** Choose comfortable shoes you can walk in on grass, sand or paths. Barefoot is lovely on clean sand or at home.
- **Extras:** Simple jewellery and soft hair movement are enough. Pack a spare top for little ones, a small towel, water and baby wipes.

## How I help you feel comfortable

You do not need to know how to pose. I offer light direction with tiny adjustments to hand placement, posture and angle to the light. We focus on movement-led prompts that feel like you, such as:

- Take a slow walk, breathe and rest a hand under your bump.
- Lean into your partner’s shoulder and close your eyes for a moment.
- Sway gently while I shift for the best light.
- Sit for a minute, soften your shoulders and relax.

I also plan around weather. On hot days we choose shade or the coast and keep to one location. On breezy afternoons, we face the wind for romantic movement or find shelter to keep hair and outfits relaxed. If the forecast looks wild, we reschedule without stress.

## Session length and what 60 minutes can cover

A full maternity session typically runs around 60 minutes so we can ease in, explore light and include partners and siblings without rushing. It may seem like a long time but it gives us room for breaks, variety and changing light.

## Quick FAQ

**What month of pregnancy is best for maternity photos?** Late second to early third trimester, commonly weeks 28 to 34, with flexibility for your comfort.

**When should I book a maternity photoshoot?** Aim to book 6 to 10 weeks ahead to secure golden hour or weekends. The earlier the better in busy seasons

**Should my partner be in maternity photos?** Absolutely. Partners and siblings are welcome, and I use gentle prompts so it all feels natural.

**Is 60 minutes enough for maternity photos?** To ensure variety and a relaxed pace, 60 minutes is a great time.

**What should I wear for a maternity photoshoot?** Movement-friendly fabrics, bump-flattering silhouettes and coordinated muted tones work best. Avoid neons and heavy logos.
